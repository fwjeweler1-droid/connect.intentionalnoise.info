OPTIONAL BAND WEBPAGE — PROPOSAL ONLY
Price idea: $12 per year for one limited public promotional page.
Keep member search, gig requests, profiles and member contact free.
Suggested page: band name, short bio, one image, music/video links, gig dates, booking link. No arbitrary scripts or unlimited media hosting.
Default example path: connect.intentionalnoise.info/myband (use a URL-safe band-name slug).
An existing domain can redirect visitors to that page. Keeping a band's own domain in the address bar requires a custom-domain hosting/routing feature plus HTTPS and domain ownership verification; DNS alone cannot target a /myband path.
myband.band is a separate registered domain under the real .band extension, not a path. Availability and registration/renewal fees are separate and not included in the proposed $12 hosting fee.
Payment collection, subscription activation, page publishing and custom-domain routing are NOT implemented or enabled. Price, limits, renewal and expiration policy need a final product decision.
