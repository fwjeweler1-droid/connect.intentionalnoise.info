What you get for free

A place to connect, create and be discovered when you're ready

Your Connect account gives you practical tools, not another subscription just to meet someone.

Make a private profile

Create your musician, band or instructor identity. Choose whether other signed-in members can find you. Public artist showcases require separate approval.

Find your people

Search listed musicians, instructors and collaborators by role, interest or location. Use IneedaGig™ for gigs and hiring.

Message members

Start private conversations with listed members. Keep existing contacts when your profile is hidden; use Block or Ignore when needed.

Show what you do

Add up to five audio/video previews, each up to 60 seconds, and links to selected work. You decide what you submit for publication.

Use member resources

Get setup guides and links for music storage, distributors, and CD/vinyl production. Save one preferred storage-provider shortcut.

Collaborate and book

Explore eligible private Studio tools and member gig requests. Venue owners can post and manage offers through the connected IneedaGig workflow.

Clear about the cost: A basic account is free, and no payment is required to register or message members. An optional, eligibility-based $12 one-time Founder price guarantee covers currently included free features; future paid add-ons and third-party charges are separate. Read the details.
