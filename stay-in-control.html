Stay in control

Your profile is private by default.

In member access, “List my profile for signed-in members” controls whether other signed-in members can discover your profile. Leave it unchecked, or uncheck it and save, to keep your profile private. Avoid putting private contact details in member-visible fields.

Member blocking, in-app reporting and additional privacy controls are planned. For a current concern or request, contact Intentional Noise.
